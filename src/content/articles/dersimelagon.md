The short answer, for anyone who searched the name hoping for a tan: **dersimelagon
is not Melanotan, and it is not for sale as one.** It is an investigational
oral drug for two rare diseases in which sunlight causes severe pain. The FDA
has not approved it. A decision is due by the end of February 2027. Nothing
on this page is a product you can order.

## What is actually in front of the FDA

On 18 September 2026, LEO Pharma said the U.S. Food and Drug Administration
had accepted a New Drug Application for dersimelagon and granted it priority
review, with a Prescription Drug User Fee Act action date by the end of
February 2027. The same announcement said LEO had closed its acquisition of
worldwide rights from Tanabe Pharma, the company that ran the development
program. Dersimelagon has not been approved by the FDA or any other
regulator. The company's own release says its safety and efficacy have not
been established by any authority.
([LEO Pharma, 18 September 2026](https://leo-pharma.com/media-center/news/leo-pharma-announces-fda-acceptance-of-dersimelagon-nda-with-priority-review-and-closes-acquisition-from-tanabe-pharma/).)

What the filing describes is narrow, and the narrowness is the point.
Dersimelagon (also called MT-7117) is a once-daily oral small molecule that
agonises the melanocortin-1 receptor — the same receptor at the centre of
[how a tan is signalled](/articles/one-hormone-five-receptors/). The proposed
use is erythropoietic protoporphyria and X-linked protoporphyria, the rare
genetic disorders in which light causes severe pain. If it is approved, it
would be the first oral treatment for those two diseases. It would not be a
tanning pill for healthy people.

## What the trial showed, and what it has not published

The application rests on INSPIRE, a global, randomised, double-blind,
placebo-controlled Phase 3 study run by Tanabe before the sale. LEO said the
study showed statistically significant and clinically meaningful results on
primary and secondary endpoints, including a longer average daily time in
sunlight before the first warning symptoms of a phototoxic reaction.

Read the next sentence as carefully as that one. The September filing
announcement did not publish effect sizes, confidence intervals, or a
detailed safety table. Dermatology Times noted the absence the day of the
release.
([Dermatology Times, 18 September 2026](https://www.dermatologytimes.com/view/dersimelagon-nda-accepted-fda-priority-review-epp-xlp).)
A sponsor saying an endpoint was met is not the same thing as a public
dataset. This page will not invent the numbers the sponsor withheld.

The [regulatory file](/regulatory/) records an earlier sponsor announcement,
in January 2026, that INSPIRE enrolled 165 patients and met its primary
endpoint. That figure lives there, dated, until a journal paper or an FDA
review document replaces it. It is not repeated here as if the September
release had confirmed it.

## What a February decision would settle

A priority-review date is a clock, not a verdict. By the end of February
2027 the FDA can approve the application, issue a complete response, or
extend the clock. Any of those is news. None of them turns dersimelagon into
the cosmetic the gray market has been selling for eighteen years.

The pattern is the one this thread keeps finding. Oral delivery is the
breakthrough the [middle-ground file](/articles/middle-ground-agonist-already-exists/)
named as missing: a pill, not a clinic implant. The indication is the same
beachhead afamelanotide already occupies — rare light-pain, not sun damage
prevented in the general public. The [class snapshot](/articles/the-pipeline-right-now/)
is where that comparison lives. This page is the query those essays were
not titled to answer.

*The future-agonist hope — a tested medicine that protects ordinary skin
from ultraviolet damage — is this site's labeled opinion, not a claim about
dersimelagon. The drug in front of the FDA is a rare-disease filing. Anyone
selling a tanning peptide under a name that sounds like it is still the
[cautionary tale](/articles/barbie-drug-gray-market/).*

This file will be updated when the agency acts, and only then. Freshness is
not a date we restamp to look current.
