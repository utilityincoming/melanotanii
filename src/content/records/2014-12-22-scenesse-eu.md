Marketing authorisation issued 22 December 2014, following a CHMP positive
opinion adopted on 23 October 2014: SCENESSE (afamelanotide 16 mg), a
subcutaneous implant, authorised for the prevention of phototoxicity in
adults with erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP). Orphan designation;
authorised under exceptional circumstances.

Afamelanotide is a melanocortin-1 receptor agonist — the same receptor
Melanotan II hits. The implant is administered by a trained health-care
professional, one implant every two months, and patients maintain sun and
light protection during treatment. It was the first systemic photoprotective
drug approved anywhere in the world, and the proof that the melanocortin
class could pass through the front door of medicine when it is developed as
a drug: a defined indication, a trial programme, a risk-management plan, a
registry.

The EPAR is the primary record of what a legitimate melanocortin approval
looks like — the standard the gray-market vial has never been near.
