The scheduling delegate's final decision of 10 April 2018 — following an
interim decision published on 5 February 2018 and a November 2017 referral
to the joint ACCS/ACMS — created a new entry for MELANOTAN II in Schedule 4
(prescription-only) of the Poisons Standard, taking effect on 1 June 2018
in the Poisons Standard June 2018 (F2018L00625). Current editions of the
Standard, including the February 2026 (F2026L00060) and October 2026
(F2026L01327) versions, still list it there.

The consequence is structural: Schedule 4 means supply requires a
prescription, and no Melanotan II product has ever been listed on the
Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods — so there has never been anything
a doctor could lawfully prescribe. Every vial, pen and spray in Australian
circulation is unlawful supply. Australia never needed a prohibition-grade
schedule; the prescription gate with no product behind it did the same work,
and left the TGA with the cleanest enforcement file in the world for MT-II.
