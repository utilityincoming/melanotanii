On 8 October 2019 the FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research approved
the NDA for SCENESSE (afamelanotide 16 mg implant), indicated "to increase
pain free light exposure in adult patients with a history of phototoxic
reactions from erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP)". The approval followed a
three-month extension of the PDUFA date on 31 May 2019, and came with seven
years of orphan-drug market exclusivity under a designation granted in 2008.

Approved dosing: a single 16 mg implant subcutaneously every two months,
administered by a health-care professional who has completed the
manufacturer's training; sun and light protection maintained during
treatment. The United States became the second jurisdiction — after the EU
in December 2014 — to approve the first systemic photoprotective drug.
