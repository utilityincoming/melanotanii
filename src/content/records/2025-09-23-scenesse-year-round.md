On 23 September 2025 the CHMP issued a positive opinion on a label variation
removing the recommended maximum annual dose of four implants per year,
allowing adult EPP patients in Europe to receive one implant every two
months year-round and harmonising the European posology with the US label.
The variation could be implemented immediately.

The committee evaluated two Phase III studies (CUV039 and CUV029) plus real-
world evidence from over 15 years of SCENESSE use under compassionate,
special-access and commercial programmes — including a review of European
patients who had received four or more implants in a single calendar year —
and concluded there were no significant safety concerns with uninterrupted
every-two-month administration. CLINUVEL's obligations under the risk-
management plan continue, including outcome data collection through the
European EPP Disease Registry.

A decade after first approval, the legitimate melanocortin is being dosed
more, not less — on reviewed evidence, with a registry attached. That is the
mechanism the gray market has never had: a dose increase that a regulator
signed.
