LegitScript's 9 December 2025 release reported multi-channel monitoring data
from 2020–2025 covering problematic peptide advertising and listings.
Advertisements increased 308% in 2024 versus 2023 — and 678% versus 2022.
Social-media content for problematic peptides rose 75% year-over-year, and
marketplace listings showed five-year growth of 276%, with 2025 on track to
exceed 2024. Melanotan and Melanotan II appeared first among the most
frequently observed product names, alongside BPC-157, TB-500, PT-141 and
GLP-1s. The release described products presented "in ways that obscure their
regulatory status".

These are counts of what a monitoring and certification vendor observed
across the channels it reviews — not incidence estimates for use, and not a
public-health dataset. LegitScript's findings feed platform and payment
policies, which is why the surge numbers moved the market: the channels they
described were the channels the sellers had migrated to, and the
enforcement ecosystem grew with them.
