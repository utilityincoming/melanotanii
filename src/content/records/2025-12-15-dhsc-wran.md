A written parliamentary question tabled on 3 December 2025 was answered by
the Department of Health and Social Care on 15 December 2025, placing the UK
regulatory split for melanotan on the official record.

Injectable Melanotan II is regarded as an unlicensed medicinal product —
presented and functioning as a medicine without a licence — so its sale and
supply are subject to medicines law. Nasal-spray formulations sold without
medicinal claims are not regarded as medicinal products and therefore fall
outside the remit of the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency.

That is the regulatory gap the gray market lives in, stated by the department
responsible for closing it: the same active substance is a medicine in a
syringe and a consumer product in a pump bottle, depending on the claims on
the label. The answer did not announce new enforcement powers; it described
the existing boundary.
