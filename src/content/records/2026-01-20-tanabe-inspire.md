On 20 January 2026 Tanabe Pharma America announced positive topline results
from INSPIRE, the global randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3
trial of dersimelagon (MT-7117) — an oral, once-daily selective MC1R agonist
— in 165 adult and adolescent (12–75 years) patients with erythropoietic
protoporphyria (EPP) or X-linked protoporphyria (XLP).

The study met its primary endpoint: time to onset of first prodromal symptoms
(burning, tingling, itching, stinging) with light exposure in the 200 mg/day
group versus placebo over the 16-week double-blind treatment period. Safety
and tolerability were reported as favorable, with most adverse events mild or
moderate; a 36-week open-label extension is ongoing.

At the time of filing the results existed as a sponsor announcement — no
peer-reviewed publication yet. Global rights subsequently passed to LEO
Pharma (see the September 2026 acquisition record and the PDUFA record). If
approved, dersimelagon becomes the first oral MC1R drug: a rare-disease
tablet, not a tanning vial — and the third proof of the class going through
the front door.
