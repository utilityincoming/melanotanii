On 24 April 2026 CLINUVEL announced that the CHMP had issued final Scientific
Advice — following over twelve months of interaction, two formal submissions
and a Discussion Meeting with the Scientific Advice Working Party — on the
design of the planned pivotal Phase III CUV107 study of SCENESSE
(afamelanotide) as a systemic treatment for non-segmental vitiligo in adults
and adolescents.

The EMA proposed evaluating efficacy on a "totality of evidence" approach,
with T-VASI50 the primary endpoint, F-VASI75 and patient-reported outcomes
assessed as secondary endpoints, and photographic evidence of change from
baseline used in the analyses. The advice prioritised patients with darker
skin colours (Fitzpatrick IV–VI), where disease visibility — the contrast
between unaffected skin and lesions — is most pronounced. The study is planned
at roughly 300 patients. Scientific advice is non-binding but is documented in
the final European public assessment report.

This is a design milestone, not efficacy data: the regulator agreeing how a
melanocortin vitiligo trial must be run before it exists. The pivotal study's
start slipped to February 2027 (scheduled record); the advice is the version
of the trial that regulators will judge.
