Published 21 May 2026: the TGA issued 27 infringement notices totalling
$101,412 to a New South Wales-based individual for the alleged unlawful
supply of Melanotan II to Australian consumers, contrary to the Therapeutic
Goods Act 1989. The notices were paid in May 2026.

The release restated the legal position it has maintained since the April
2018 scheduling decision: Melanotan II is a prescription-only substance, it
is not approved as a tanning agent, no Melanotan II product is listed on the
Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods, and it may not lawfully be supplied
in any dosage form — spray, tablet, injection or cream — without a
prescription. Public advertising of prescription-only medicines is itself an
offence.

This is the largest published monetary enforcement figure attached to a
melanotan supplier anywhere, and the cleanest demonstration of why the
Schedule 4 structure works: no ban needed, no court needed — one seller, 27
notices, one payment. The same seller's products were back in a TGA safety
alert three months later (the 17 August 2026 dosing record).
