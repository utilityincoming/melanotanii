Health Canada issued a Notice of Compliance for SCENESSE (afamelanotide
16 mg implant) on 9 July 2026 — authorising it to be marketed in Canada for
the prevention of phototoxicity in adult patients with erythropoietic
protoporphyria (EPP), following review under sections C.08.002 and C.08.005
of the Food and Drug Regulations. CLINUVEL announced the approval on
13 July 2026.

Canada became the fourth jurisdiction to approve the first systemic
photoprotective drug, after the European Union (December 2014), the United
States (October 2019) and Australia (2020). Five Canadian specialist centres
were already accredited for implantation; until the NOC, Canadian EPP
patients received SCENESSE through the Special Access Programme.

The pattern across all four approvals is the one the Record tracks: the
melanocortin class keeps passing through the front door of medicine — orphan
indication, trial programme, trained administration, pharmacovigilance —
while the substance the gray market sells for a tan remains unapproved in
every one of these countries.
