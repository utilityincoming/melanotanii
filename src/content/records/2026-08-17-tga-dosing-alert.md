On 17 August 2026 the TGA issued a safety alert after products seized during
an investigation were confirmed by TGA laboratory testing to contain
Melanotan II. Assay of five nasal-spray bottles labelled "Pure Tans Triple
Strength 30 MG" found estimated contents ranging from 22 mg to 54 mg per
bottle, based on the labelled volume of 20 mL.

The alert's own words: "The inconsistent dosing across bottles further
increases the chance of adverse events arising from the use of these
products." The seller was the same New South Wales individual who received
27 infringement notices totalling $101,412 in May 2026 — notices that were
paid, and three months later whose products were still being tested by the
regulator.

The alert restated the standing position: Melanotan II is prescription-only
in Australia, no product is registered on the ARTG, and it is unlawful to
supply in any dosage form. What makes this record is the number pair —
22 mg versus 54 mg in identically labelled bottles: the "dose" on the label
is decoration, and nobody downstream knows what they are administering.
