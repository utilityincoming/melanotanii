A Federal Register notice published on 30 October 2026 (document
2026-22192), "Enforcement Discretion Policy Regarding 503A Compounding Using
Certain Peptide Bulk Drug Substances", sets out which peptide bulk drug
substances the FDA will exercise enforcement discretion over for 503A
compounding pharmacies.

Melanotan II is not among them. Having been withdrawn from Category 2 in
April 2026 and deferred by the Pharmaceutical Compounding Advisory Committee
in July 2026 — for insufficient data on safety, efficacy and chemical
characterisation — it remains ineligible for compounding under any current
FDA policy. The advisory committee is expected to revisit Melanotan II
before the end of February 2027 (scheduled record).

The interim policy is the clearest US statement of where the substance
stands: not banned by name, not approved, not compoundable — parked outside
every framework, with the only route back running through the advisory
calendar.
