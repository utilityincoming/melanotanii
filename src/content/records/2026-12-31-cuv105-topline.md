Scheduled: at the Annual General Meeting on 6 November 2026, CLINUVEL's
Managing Director said topline results from the Phase III CUV105 study —
afamelanotide implant combined with narrow-band UVB versus narrow-band UVB
alone in vitiligo — will be released in December 2026. The study has enrolled
210 patients.

This record exists so the site can file the outcome when it arrives, whatever
it is: the topline is a company announcement, not peer-reviewed data, and the
Record's habit is to mark that difference at the moment of the calendar entry
rather than after the fact.

Status moves to filed when the readout is published.
