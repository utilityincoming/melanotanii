Scheduled: the pivotal Phase III CUV107 study of afamelanotide (SCENESSE) as
a systemic vitiligo treatment slipped by three months — protocol validation
took longer than planned — moving first-patient-first-visit from November
2026 to February 2027, per CLINUVEL's address to the Annual General Meeting
on 6 November 2026.

The design is the one the EMA signed off in its April 2026 final scientific
advice (filed record): roughly 300 patients, adults and adolescents with
non-segmental vitiligo, Fitzpatrick IV–VI prioritised, T-VASI50 the primary
endpoint.

A start date is a calendar commitment, not data — this record moves to filed
when enrolment actually opens, or gets amended again if it slips.
