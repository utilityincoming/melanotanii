Scheduled: on 18 September 2026 LEO Pharma announced it had completed the
acquisition of global rights to dersimelagon (MT-7117) from Tanabe Pharma,
and that the FDA had accepted the New Drug Application with priority review
for erythropoietic protoporphyria and X-linked protoporphyria. The PDUFA
action date is by the end of February 2027.

The submission rests on the INSPIRE Phase III results announced by Tanabe on
20 January 2026 (filed record): 165 patients, primary endpoint met — longer
time to first prodromal symptoms of light exposure versus placebo on a
once-daily 200 mg oral dose.

This is a deadline for a decision, not a decision. If approved, dersimelagon
becomes the first oral MC1R agonist on the market — a rare-disease tablet,
and a demonstration of how far the legitimate melanocortin class has moved
from an unlicensed vial. The record moves to filed on the FDA's answer,
either way.
