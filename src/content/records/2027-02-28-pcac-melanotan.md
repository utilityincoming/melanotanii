Scheduled: the Pharmaceutical Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) deferred
Melanotan II at its July 2026 meeting, citing insufficient data on safety,
efficacy and chemical characterisation — alongside injectable GHK-Cu,
dihexa acetate, cathelicidin LL-37 and PEG-MGF. A session addressing the
503A bulk-substance list question is expected before the end of February
2027; no meeting date had been set at the time of filing.

This matters because it is the first time the substance's US legitimacy
question sits on a formal calendar: the October 2026 interim policy excludes
MT-II from enforcement discretion (filed record), and the advisory committee
is the route back in — if a nominator can ever assemble the safety, efficacy
and characterisation data the committee said were missing.

The record moves to filed when the committee meets, whatever it concludes.
